# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

FFmpeg Media Toolkit is designed to process media in the user's own environment.
Cecil-IA Labs does not require users to upload media files to Cecil-IA Labs in
order to use the toolkit.

If you contact support or submit an issue, we may receive the information you
choose to provide, such as your GitHub account details, diagnostic output, and
message content. We use this information only to respond, maintain the project,
and address reliability or security concerns. We do not sell this information.

GitHub, npm, FFmpeg, and any other third-party services or tools you use are
governed by their own privacy policies. Avoid including secrets, personal data,
or sensitive media in public reports.

To request help with information submitted to this project, open a
[support issue](https://github.com/cecil-ia-labs/ffmpeg-dev/issues/new). Updates
to this policy will be published in this file.
